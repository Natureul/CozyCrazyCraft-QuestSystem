# CozyCrazyCraft Quest System 0.4.1 — Integration Status

Date: 2026-09-07

Branch: `playtest-hardening-0.4.1`

Authority: this document describes the current integrated 0.4.1 branch. `docs/qa/0.4.1-regression-hardening-report.md` is retained as the earlier isolated QA-branch handoff and should not be read as the current defect list.

## Release posture

0.4.1 is now in **integration acceptance / in-game verification**, not architecture construction.

The September 0.4.0 field failures have implementation guards in place for structure proof, recovery identity, social/civic routing, navigation knowledge, recent quest novelty, hint repetition, expensive target resolution and HUD prose. The remaining work is primarily to prove those rules in a real generated world and tune cases where technically correct geometry still feels bad to a player.

`data/qa_regression_matrix.json` is the machine-readable acceptance matrix. `GUARDED` means a source/runtime invariant protects the failure class. `MANUAL` means the implementation exists but world or UX quality still requires an in-game pass.

## What changed from 0.4.0

### Structure surveys

- `VillageConversationQuestManager` no longer owns a locator-radius survey tick.
- `StructureSurveyCompletionBridge` is the authored survey-completion authority.
- Exact proof requires the assigned generated `StructureStart`, an actual structure-piece bounding box, and qualifying dwell.
- Surface X/Z proximity is not completion proof.
- Underground and submerged work cannot complete from standing above a target.
- Whole-start bounding boxes cannot prove occupancy through empty gaps between pieces.

### Recovery jobs

- Structure occupancy does not mint evidence.
- Recovery begins through a real container interaction in the exact assigned structure instance.
- Evidence is a quest-bound item keyed to quest, village and target.
- Objective completion waits until the player actually possesses that evidence.
- Turn-in consumes the matching evidence.
- A full target cache does not silently teleport evidence into the player's inventory; the player is asked to free cache space and interact again.
- Display-name lookalikes cannot satisfy recovery identity.

### Atlas and knowledge

The runtime preserves the Conversation Bible ladder:

`UNKNOWN -> RUMOR -> LEAD -> KNOWN -> CONFIRMED`

- RUMOR and LEAD cannot reveal an exact target pin.
- Dynamic route feedback is compact (`Rumor: ~400 NW`, `Lead: ~300 NW`) rather than sentence-sized HUD prose.
- KNOWN may reveal an Atlas marker when the conversation establishes reliable knowledge.
- Underground/submerged KNOWN markers use a derived surface approach/structure edge rather than an interior chamber center.
- Objective identity and navigation-anchor geometry are separate concepts.

### Tunnel Gore

- Gore remains a bespoke knowledge/location reward, not casual hunt fodder.
- The Atlas marker is a surface approach, not the lair center.
- CONFIRMED discovery requires dwell in a real piece of the exact Gore structure instance.
- Broad discovery radius fallback is gone.
- Gore social dialogue is now refreshable, so advancing the rumor/lead state cannot leave the informing villager permanently stuck on an earlier conversation page.

The remaining manual question is **quality**: does the generated surface-edge marker feel like a useful tunnel approach in actual worlds? If not, the next improvement should be piece/entrance-specific Gore navigation rather than relaxing discovery proof.

### Jobless, tiny and damaged villages

- Civic/adventure roles are independent of vanilla trade professions.
- The deterministic first adults become steward, roadwarden, quartermaster and watch contact while their vanilla profession/workstation/trades remain untouched.
- One surviving adult therefore still has a steward role; two adults expose a second civic surface.
- Civic contacts can expose village work when no profession holders exist.
- Civic contacts can present active and turn-in dialogue for village-owned contracts if the original profession-specific giver is gone.
- `route_help` routes through current progression state, profession contacts when available, then civic contacts.
- Referrals reject villagers from a different village key.

### Hint repetition and villager sameness

The current runtime now has two distinct anti-repeat layers:

1. Profession/civic voice resources retain stable speaker identity rather than rerolling a personality every click.
2. `VillageQuestState` stores the last five **semantic hint families** per player/village. `QuestHintNetwork` prefers an appropriate family not recently used before repeating one.

Examples:
- a mason still prefers stone/foundation evidence;
- a librarian/cleric still prefers records;
- a route specialist prefers practical approach knowledge;
- generic residents rotate deterministically among unknown, rumor, referral and lead families, with recent-family suppression layered on top.

Once a target is KNOWN, profession-aware responses remain available instead of every resident collapsing to the same single `Known Place` page.

### Stale Conversations state

Conversations IDs live on entities, so an ambient/social page could otherwise remain attached after world or quest state changed.

0.4.1 now distinguishes refreshable social pages from authored quest pages:

- refreshable: normal villager chatter, children, civic ambient pages, hints, Guard Villager local pages and Gore social stages;
- sticky: authored quest offer/active/turn-in pages selected by the higher-priority quest managers.

This lets a Gore rumor become a later-stage Gore conversation, lets stale completed-task hints disappear, and lets child/civic/ambient state be recalculated without wiping a real quest conversation.

### Quest novelty

- Recent completion state remembers target instance and primary reward family per village/player.
- The next authored offer down-ranks the exact just-used structure instance unless the content is an intentional continuation.
- Back-to-back primary reward families are down-ranked.
- Amber Rest's 0.4.0 failure is also fixed at the content level: the stable-ledger recovery work no longer simply sends the player straight back to the same stables for another saddle-shaped reward.

### Performance boundary

- Authored villager interaction does not call Minecraft's synchronous broad `findNearestMapStructure` path.
- `GeneratedStructureIndexSavedData` records real generated starts as chunks load and persists them.
- NPC target selection searches that bounded generated-start index.
- If a suitable landmark has not actually generated/been indexed, a structure-dependent offer simply is not available yet.
- The September watchdog dump remains classified by its actual server-thread stack; the captured severe stall was a teleport/chunk-load stall, not retroactively blamed on quest code.

## UI contract for the next playtest

### Conversations

Use for:
- NPC explanation;
- uncertainty;
- why a person knows something;
- warnings;
- referrals and social context;
- quest offer/active/turn-in prose.

### Action bar

Use for compact transient data/state only, for example:
- `Survey complete: <place>.`
- `Recovered: <object>.`
- `Rumor: ~400 NW`
- `Lead: ~300 NW`
- `<name> • mason • 80 NW`
- `Atlas marked.`
- `Board • 35 E`

Do not put a second NPC speech paragraph in the action bar after a Conversations reply.

### Chat

Reserve for durable system events where persistence is useful: contract acceptance/completion and exceptional recovery/error state. Normal NPC speech does not belong here.

## Next in-game acceptance route

A fresh or disposable generated world is preferred. Creative/spectator can be used to accelerate travel, but structure completion should also be checked in ordinary survival movement because spectator can cross geometry in ways a normal player cannot.

1. **Surface survey** — accept a stables/house/ruin survey. Walk inside the old radius but remain outside actual pieces. It must not complete. Enter a real piece and remain briefly; it should complete once.
2. **Underground survey** — stand directly above target X/Z. Nothing should complete. Enter a real underground piece and dwell; then it should complete.
3. **Recovery** — enter assigned structure without opening containers: no evidence. Open a real eligible cache: evidence should appear there. Take it: objective completes. Return: exact evidence disappears on successful turn-in.
4. **Recovery full-cache edge** — fill an eligible target cache before opening it. The evidence must not appear magically in inventory. Free one cache slot and retry.
5. **Five-villager hint sweep** — ask at least five residents about one target. Record the actual conversation titles/families. They should not all collapse to one generic answer, and profession evidence should remain recognizable.
6. **KNOWN map request** — obtain reliable specialist knowledge and request a mark. Verify exact target/approach behavior. RUMOR/LEAD must never have pinned it earlier.
7. **Gore** — obtain the bespoke lead. Verify the Atlas point is a surface reference. Approach the broad lair area without entering a piece: no confirmation. Enter a real tunnel/lair piece and dwell: confirmation should occur. Judge whether the surface marker was genuinely useful.
8. **Jobless village** — find or create an immature settlement with unemployed adults. Without placing workstations, verify civic conversations and a useful progression route exist.
9. **Tiny/successor village** — leave one or two adults or remove the original quest giver. Village-owned active/turn-in routing should remain possible through civic contacts.
10. **Novelty** — complete one structure quest, then immediately seek another. The system should avoid the same exact structure/reward family unless the content explicitly justifies a continuation.
11. **UI pass** — watch every Conversations choice and resulting HUD/chat message. Flag anything that feels like NPC prose appearing outside the conversation panel.
12. **Performance pass** — click quest-capable residents in several newly explored villages while watching tick behavior. NPC interaction should not trigger broad structure generation/locate stalls.

## Ship decision

Do not call 0.4.1 final solely because CI is green. CI now protects the implementation shape that 0.4.0 lacked; the next meaningful evidence is one focused generated-world acceptance pass against the twelve checks above.

If those checks pass, remaining changes should be tuning/content polish rather than another architectural rewrite.
