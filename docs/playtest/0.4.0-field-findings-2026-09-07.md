# CozyCrazyCraft Quest System 0.4.0 — Field Playtest Findings

Date: 2026-09-07

Status: P0/P1 hardening authority for the 0.4.1 cycle.

This document records the first substantial player-facing problems observed in the 0.4.0 integrated playtest. Treat these as systemic UX/runtime defects before adding large amounts of new executable quest content.

## Executive diagnosis

0.4.0 proved that the authored-village architecture is viable, but several implementation shortcuts are visible to the player. The next release should prioritize trustworthy completion semantics, conversation-first information delivery, better underground navigation, village role availability, and quest/reward novelty.

## P0 — Structure completion is too permissive

Observed:
- `hearthlands_community_empty_stalls` could report survey completion before the player felt they had actually reached/seen the assigned Dungeons Enhanced stables.
- The same problem is especially dangerous for underground structures and Tunnel Gore, where a large structure bounding box is not equivalent to entering a meaningful room or discovering an entrance.

Current implementation risk:
- surface surveys have a horizontal-distance fallback around a located target;
- `NamedPlaceBridge.insideExactStructure(...)` currently treats inclusion in the whole generated structure bounding box as exact occupancy;
- `StructureSurveyCompletionBridge` can therefore confirm surveys before meaningful visual/physical discovery.

Required direction:
- remove broad radial completion for authored structure surveys;
- distinguish `structure bounding box`, `actual structure piece`, `approach/discovery`, and `meaningful interior occupancy`;
- require an actual discovery signal or tighter piece-level presence, preferably with a short dwell requirement where practical;
- underground/submerged work must never complete from a surface X/Z match alone.

## P0 — Recovery evidence should be a real thing the player finds

Observed:
- the stable-ledger quest felt as if it completed automatically around the site;
- the player later saw a named ledger-like item in a chest, brought it back, and it remained afterward, making it unclear what the quest had actually accepted.

Log evidence shows the quest runtime generated recovered evidence automatically from exact-structure occupancy. That makes the backend technically deterministic but the player-facing fiction unclear.

Required direction:
- stop granting recovery evidence merely for entering a structure/bounding box;
- prefer placing/binding the quest evidence to a real eligible container in the exact assigned structure instance;
- if generic container injection is unsafe for a given structure, use a clearly communicated interactable/cache fallback rather than invisible inventory generation;
- turn-in must visibly correspond to the object the player believes they recovered;
- add validation/instrumentation for duplicate/foreign similarly named loot.

## P0 — Long prose belongs in Conversations, not the action bar

Observed:
- hint/referral actions repeatedly produced long, samey, immersion-breaking popups;
- some text exceeded the practical size of the HUD format;
- useful information often appeared after a Conversations choice as a separate system popup rather than as the villager's speech.

Required UI contract:
- Conversations pages: explanation, directions, referrals, uncertainty, map knowledge, warnings, quest clarification;
- action bar: only very short transient confirmations, e.g. `Survey complete: Amber Rest.` or `Stable Ledger recovered.`;
- chat: durable/system events only when needed, not ordinary NPC prose;
- avoid presenting one semantic exchange through multiple UI systems unless each surface has a distinct purpose.

The Conversation Bible short-beat limits apply to all authored NPC prose.

## P0/P1 — Knowledge-sensitive navigation needs to become real

Current intended ladder remains:
`UNKNOWN -> RUMOR -> LEAD -> KNOWN -> CONFIRMED`.

Navigation policy for 0.4.1:
- UNKNOWN: no fabricated direction; refer to someone who may know.
- RUMOR: rough direction/search area; no exact Atlas pin.
- LEAD: route description plus a known intermediate landmark/approach when available.
- KNOWN: a knowledgeable villager may mark the destination/approach precisely on the Atlas.
- CONFIRMED: acknowledge player discovery; do not redundantly reveal it again.

For underground targets, `KNOWN` should generally mark a safe surface approach, shaft/entrance, or structure edge—not the boss/chamber center. If no verified entrance exists, derive a conservative approach marker on/near the structure edge closest to the issuing village and clearly call it a surface reference.

## P0/P1 — Tunnel Gore needs bespoke discovery/marker semantics

Observed:
- the Tunnel Gore information reward itself felt good;
- discovery fired before the player felt meaningfully close;
- the Atlas marker landed too close to/on the Gore instead of giving a safe tunnel reference.

Required:
- preserve Gore as an authored location/knowledge reward, never casual kill fodder;
- do not confirm discovery from the overall lair bounding box alone;
- use a safer tunnel/approach marker rather than generic structure-center marker;
- if exact entrance geometry can be verified, use it; otherwise bias to the nearest safe edge/approach from the informing village.

## P1 — Generated villages must never be socially inert

Observed:
- a newly visited village had essentially no useful quest-capable villagers because residents had not claimed professions/job sites.

Do not solve this by blindly mutating vanilla professions or inventing job sites. Instead introduce village-owned civic/adventure roles that can be assigned to existing adult residents independently of vanilla trade profession, e.g. steward, roadwarden, quartermaster, local witness, survey contact. These roles are progression/social roles, not trade professions.

Minimum target:
- every normal village with enough living adult villagers should expose multiple conversational surfaces immediately;
- Zone 1 should normally have 3–6 residents able to surface at least two starter problems or useful referrals;
- if population is tiny, public/civic fallback routing must still prevent an empty village experience.

## P1 — Quest novelty and reward coherence

Observed Amber Stalls sequence:
1. survey the stables;
2. receive a saddle;
3. get another authored job at the same stables for a ledger;
4. receive another saddle-oriented reward.

The individual quests are plausible, but the sequence feels generated rather than authored.

Required selection guardrails:
- strongly down-weight the exact same structure instance immediately after a completed quest unless the next quest is an explicit authored continuation that acknowledges the previous visit;
- if a follow-up uses the same place, dialogue must explain why new information/work emerged;
- do not repeat the same primary reward family back-to-back for the same player/village chapter;
- first discovery, recovery follow-up, combat clear, and lore follow-up should have distinct reward identities;
- local histories should remember recently used targets/reward archetypes.

## P1 — Dialogue variation is an implementation problem, not only a writing-volume problem

The current Conversation Bible already supports personality, profession, region, trust, knowledge provenance, local facts, weather/event state, and named-world references. Implement those dimensions instead of adding thousands of flat interchangeable lines.

Desired composition dimensions:
`region x tier x profession/civic-role x personality x trust x current local fact x knowledge provenance x recent-dialogue history`.

Anti-repeat:
- track a small recent line/variant history per villager/player or village/player;
- do not let every villager repeat the same semantic hint wording;
- the same fact may be expressed differently by a mason, farmer, cartographer, cautious villager, veteran, etc.

## Performance observation

The playtest log also contains expensive structure-locate/Structure Gel paths and at least one severe integrated-server watchdog stall. Quest target resolution should be profiled/cached and must not repeatedly force expensive broad structure checks on routine villager interactions. Treat this as a separate QA/performance lane from the UX fixes above.

## 0.4.1 release acceptance tests

Before calling 0.4.1 playable:
- a surface survey cannot complete from merely crossing a generous radius outside the visible structure;
- an underground survey cannot complete while standing above its X/Z footprint;
- recovery evidence is obtained through an understandable physical interaction and is consumed on turn-in;
- hint prose fits inside Conversations and action-bar messages remain short;
- KNOWN locations can be precisely marked when fictionally justified;
- underground/Gore markers use safe approach semantics;
- an immature/jobless normal village still has useful civic quest/referral surfaces;
- two sequential authored quests do not accidentally send the player to the same structure for the same reward family without explicit continuation writing;
- two nearby villages do not cross-route social hints;
- structure target resolution does not produce pathological repeated locate costs during ordinary conversation.
